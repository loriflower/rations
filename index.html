📋 AGREEMENTS & REGULATIONS

  • Durability: Rations must last at least 2 years in standard conditions.
  • Climate Adaptation: In extreme climates, shelf life is reduced to 9 months.

🇮🇹 ITALIAN MILITARY RATIONS

The Italian rations are divided into 7 modules (A to G).

  • Breakfast: Biscuits, milk, and coffee.
  • Lunch & Dinner: Pasta, meat, and fruit.

🇺🇸 US MEAL READY-TO-EAT (MRE)

There are 24 variations and they provide a caloric intake of 1300k. They can be stored for 2 years or 9 months if used in extremely hot or extremely cold climates. MRE 23 provides approximately 1400 calories, unlike others military rations, it is a single meal.

Technical Specifications

The MRE can be consumed without fire using the FRH system (magnesium, iron, and salt reaction). This avoids revealing the soldiers' position.
