📋 AGREEMENTS & REGULATIONS

STANAG 2937 (NATO)

Adopted in 2001, the Standardization Agreement ensures that soldiers from different NATO countries receive adequate and safe nutrition during joint operations.

  • Energy Intake: Rations must provide between 3,600 and 4,900 kcal per day (depending on activity).
  • Fat Content: Fats must not exceed 30% of the total energy intake.
  • Nutritional Balance: Menus must be varied to maintain physical and mental performance.

HACCP & Technical Safety

All military food must strictly follow the HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points) system to eliminate biological, chemical, or physical risks.

Technical Durability Requirements:
  • Temperature Resistance: Must remain stable and safe from -30°C to +60°C.
  • Shelf Life: Guaranteed for at least 2 to 3 years in any weather condition.
  • Sterilization: Advanced heat or pressure treatments ensure safety without refrigeration.

🇮🇹 RAZIONE VIVERI SPECIALE DA COMBATTIMENTO

The Italian ration is a complete 24-hour food supply designed for soldiers in operational environments.

Modular Structure

It is divided into 7 different modules, identified by specific colors (Module A to Module G). This variety is essential to prevent "dietary boredom" (monotonia alimentare) and ensure balanced nutrition over long periods.

Equipment and Hygiene

Inside each package, the soldier finds survival and hygiene tools:

  • Disposable folding stove and fuel tablets.
  • Water purification tablets to make water safe.
  • Hygiene kit: tissues, toothbrushes, and more.
  • A small dose of alcohol ("Cordiale").
Average Energy Intake: Approximately 3,000 kcal per day, optimized for high physical exertion.

🇺🇸 MEAL, READY-TO-EAT (MRE)

The MRE is the primary individual combat ration of the U.S. Armed Forces, designed for consumption in the field.

Operational Analysis

Pros:
  • Highly portable and durable.
  • Great variety (24 different menus).
  • Includes the "Flameless Ration Heater" (FRH) for hot meals without fire.
  • Provides a psychological boost through familiar snacks.
Cons:
  • Very high sodium (salt) levels.
  • High levels of saturated fats and preservatives.

🇷🇺 RUSSIAN MILITARY RATIONS

Rations tailored to specific mission profiles and harsh environments.

Main Classifications

  • IRP: The standard daily ration for personnel.
  • MRGP: Specialized for mountain combat and cold climates.
  • SHTURM: Compact and light for assault or emergency operations.
