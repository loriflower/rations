Summary Overview

Military logistics require food that is durable, high-calorie, and easy to prepare in hostile environments.

📋 AGREEMENTS & REGULATIONS

Strict Suitability Standards

Every military food item is strictly regulated. The Ministry of Defense ensures that each ration is perfectly suitable for combat needs, providing specific nutrition while remaining safe for long-term storage.

General Technical Resilience:
  • Durability: Rations must last at least 2 years in standard conditions.
  • Climate Adaptation: In extremely hot or cold climates, shelf life is often reduced (e.g., to 9 months).
  • Packaging: Must resist UV rays, water, and impact.

🇮🇹 ITALIAN MILITARY RATIONS

The 7 Modules (A to G)

The Italian rations are divided into 7 specific modules, classified from A to G. Each module is identified by a different color and contains different foods to avoid food monotony.

Daily Nutritional Structure:
  • Breakfast: Sweet foods like biscuits or jelly, milk, and freeze-dried coffee.
  • Lunch & Dinner: Ready-made meals (pasta, minestrone) and proteins (turkey, meat in jelly).
  • Additional items: Fruit and coffee.

Equipment

  • A small cooking stove.
  • Personal cutlery.
  • A water disinfection kit.

🇺🇸 US MEAL READY-TO-EAT (MRE)

The US military ration is called the MRE (Meal Ready to Eat).

There are 24 variations and they provide a caloric intake of 1300k. They can be stored for 2 years or 9 months if used in extremely hot or extremely cold climates. MRE 23 provides approximately 1400 calories, unlike others military rations, it is a single meal.

The FRH System

MREs use a chemical compound called FRH (Flameless Ration Heater). This works through a reaction between magnesium, iron, and salt, which is very useful because it does not reveal the position of the soldiers.

🇷🇺 RUSSIAN MILITARY RATIONS

The 3 Types:
  • IRP (Daily Ration): Used every day by normal soldiers.
  • MRGP: Made for the hard climate of Russia’s winter and mountains.
  • SHTURM: Made for quick missions or emergency situations.

It provides approximately 3,000 kcal divided between breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
