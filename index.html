🇮🇹 RAZIONE VIVERI SPECIALE DA COMBATTIMENTO

The Italian ration is a complete 24-hour food supply designed for soldiers in operational environments.

Modular Structure

It is divided into 7 different modules, identified by specific colors (Module A to Module G). This variety is essential to prevent "dietary boredom" and ensure the soldier receives different nutrients and flavors over long periods.

Equipment and Hygiene

Inside each package, the soldier finds not only food but also survival tools:

  • Disposable folding stove and fuel tablets.
  • Water purification tablets (to make non-potable water safe).
  • Tissues, toothbrushes, and hygiene kits.
  • A small dose of alcohol (Cordiale).
Average Energy Intake: Approximately 3,000 kcal per day, balanced for high physical activity.

🇺🇸 MEAL, READY-TO-EAT (MRE)

The MRE is the main individual combat ration of the U.S. Armed Forces, designed for consumption in the field when organized food service is not available.

Analysis

Pros:
  • Extremely easy to transport and durable.
  • Very high variety (24 different menus).
  • Includes the "Flameless Ration Heater" (FRH) to heat food without a fire.
  • High psychological boost due to familiar snacks.

Cons:
  • Very high levels of sodium (salt).
  • Contains many saturated fats and preservatives for long shelf life.

🇷🇺 RUSSIAN MILITARY RATIONS

The Russian Federation uses various rations tailored to specific combat conditions and environmental challenges.

Main Types

  • IRP: The standard daily ration for the average soldier.
  • MRGP: Specialized for mountain warfare and extremely cold climates.
  • SHTURM: Compact and light, used during assault or emergency maneuvers.

📋 AGREEMENTS & REGULATIONS

STANAG 2937 (NATO)

Adopted in 2001, the STANAG (Standardization Agreement) ensures that soldiers from different NATO countries receive adequate and safe nutrition during joint operations.

  • Energy: Rations must provide between 3,600 and 4,900 kcal per day.
  • Fat: Fats should not exceed 30% of the total energy intake.
  • Variety: Menus must be varied to maintain the physical and mental health of the personnel.

HACCP & Technical Durability

All military food must strictly follow the HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points) system to eliminate biological, chemical, or physical risks.

Technical Storage Requirements:
  • Temperature Resistance: Must remain stable and safe from -30°C to +60°C.
  • Shelf Life: Guaranteed for at least 2 to 3 years in any weather condition.
  • Sterilization: Food is often treated with high pressure or heat to ensure total safety without refrigeration.
