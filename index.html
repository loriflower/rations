📋 AGREEMENTS & REGULATIONS

NATO STANAG 2937

Adopted to standardize nutritional requirements across member nations. It ensures every soldier receives adequate energy in the field.

  • Calories: 3,600 to 4,900 kcal per day.
  • Proteins: 10% to 12% of total energy.
  • Fats: Maximum 30% of energy.

HACCP STANDARDS

The Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points system ensures that rations are sterile and chemically safe.

Technical Resilience:
  • Thermal Range: Stable from -30°C to +60°C.
  • Storage: Minimum 24-36 months without refrigeration.
  • Packaging: Water-resistant and impact-resistant.

🇮🇹 ITALIAN "RAZIONE SPECIALE"

Modules A to G

The Italian army uses 7 different modules, each identified by a color. This variety prevents "dietary fatigue" and ensures balanced nutrition over a week.

Inside the Kit:
  • A folding metal stove with fuel tablets.
  • Water purification tablets (Chlorine based).
  • Complete hygiene kit: 3 toothbrushes + paste.
  • The "Cordiale": A 3cl dose of alcohol for morale and warmth.

Nutritional Focus

The Italian ration is famous for its quality, including pasta and meat courses that follow strict Mediterranean ratios of macronutrients.

🇺🇸 US MEAL READY-TO-EAT (MRE)

The Science of FRH

The Flameless Ration Heater uses a chemical reaction: $$Mg + 2H_2O \rightarrow Mg(OH)_2 + H_2 + \text{heat}$$ By adding a small amount of water to the magnesium/iron pad, it reaches boiling temperatures in seconds without smoke.

🇷🇺 RUSSIAN INDIVIDUAL RATIONS

Specific Classifications

  • IRP (Individualniy Ratsion Pitaniya): The standard daily pack.
  • MRGP: Mountain/Winter specialized ration with extra fat.
  • SHTURM: Assault ration, smaller and lighter for 1 meal.

IRP Composition

Designed for the harsh Russian winter, these rations are incredibly high in calories to prevent hypothermia.

  • Beef Stew (High fat content)
  • Lard / Pork fat spread
  • Hard wheat crackers (very high carb density)
