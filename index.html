🇮🇹 ITALIAN COMBAT RATION

The Italian ration, known as "Razione Viveri Speciale da Combattimento", is organized into 7 different modules (A to G), identified by colors to prevent dietary boredom during long missions.

Key Features:
- Average energy intake: ~3,000 kcal
- Components: 3 full meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)
- Extras: Includes a small dose of "Cordiale" (alcohol) and a folding stove with fuel tablets.

📋 AGREEMENTS & REGULATIONS

NATO STANAG 2937

Adopted in 2001, this agreement defines the minimum nutritional requirements for NATO member countries' rations.

  • Caloric Range: Between 3,600 and 4,900 kcal depending on activity level.
  • Fat Content: Must not exceed 30% of the total energy.

HACCP & SAFETY

Military rations must comply with HACCP standards to ensure food safety in extreme environments.

Durability: Must be edible for 2-3 years.
Temperature Resistance: Guaranteed stability from -30°C to +60°C.
